# Date

The Date renderer detects an **ISO 8601 subset**: full extended-form calendar dates
(`YYYY-MM-DD`) and RFC 3339 datetimes, with or without a trailing timezone offset.
It renders dates in a user-friendly, locale-aware format. Basic, ordinal, and
week-date calendar forms are not supported, and a datetime without a timezone
(such as `2023-01-18T09:21:00`) is treated as local time rather than as a
timezone-qualified RFC 3339 timestamp. Dates are simple types with no expandable details.

## Supported Formats

- Full ISO 8601: `2022-11-11T08:01:20.557+00:00`
- Date only: `2024-06-15`
- Date with time: `2024-06-15T09:30:00.000+02:00`
- Local date with time (no timezone): `2024-06-15T09:30:00`

Reduced-precision dates (`2023`, `2023-01`) are handled by the `ReducedDateType` renderer,
and ISO 8601 durations (e.g. `P7DT2H`) by the `DurationType` renderer.

## Settings

No additional settings are required. The renderer uses the browser's locale defaults for formatting.
